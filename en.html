Privacy Policy for Tank Princess: Kill All

Last updated: October 4, 2026. Versione italiana

This policy explains what data the Android game Tank Princess: Kill All ("the Game") processes and how it is used. The data controller is Mirko Fusato, who can be reached at tankprincess.support@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • The Game does not require an account, registration, name or email.
  • Your game progress (coins, upgrades, unlocked stages, settings) stays on your device only.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect certain device data.
  • The Game is not intended for children under 13.

2. Data stored on your device

The Game saves locally the information it needs to work: coins, upgrade levels, unlocked stages, attempts, records and preferences (for example music on or off). This data is not sent to any server operated by the controller. If you uninstall the Game or clear the app data in Android settings, it is deleted and cannot be recovered.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses Google AdMob to show banners and optional rewarded videos (for example to double coins or continue a run). Google, as the service provider, may collect and use:

  • the device advertising ID;
  • the IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device information (model, operating system, language);
  • data about interactions with ads (views, clicks).

This data is used to serve ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud. For more information see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can limit ad personalization in your Android settings (Google, Ads) and reset or delete your advertising ID at any time.

4. Consent (EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game shows a consent form on first launch, managed through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can accept, decline or manage your choices. If you do not consent, only non-personalized ads may be shown. You can change your choice at any time from the Game's privacy options, if available, or by clearing the app data.

5. In-app purchases

The Game may offer in-app purchases (for example removing ads or unlocking features). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play: the controller does not receive or store payment card details. To verify purchases, the Game may use the purchase status information provided by Google Play.

6. Data we do not collect

The Game does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, precise location or other direct personal data, and does not sell data to third parties.

7. Purposes and legal basis

  • Operating the Game (local saving): performance of the service requested by the user.
  • Personalized advertising: user consent, where required by law.
  • Non-personalized advertising, measurement and fraud prevention: legitimate interest.

8. Retention

Local data remains on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear the app data. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

9. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children of that age. If you believe a child has provided data through the Game, contact us at the address above and we will look into it.

10. Your rights

Under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of or objection to processing, and data portability, and you may withdraw consent at any time. Since the controller does not store personal data on its own servers, for data processed by Google AdMob you can exercise your rights directly with Google. For any request write to tankprincess.support@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Italy: the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it).

11. Security

The Game takes reasonable measures to protect the data saved on your device. The security of the device remains the user's responsibility.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated, for example when new features are added. The current version is always the one published at this address, with the update date at the top.
